Built-in track
==============

Tuneable looks for a file called sample.mp3 in this folder:

    public/audio/sample.mp3

If it exists, the "Built-in track" option plays it. If it does not, the app
renders a short demo track in the browser instead (see src/audio/demo.ts), so
everything still works out of the box.

Use a royalty-free track you have the rights to ship, such as one released under
CC0 or a licence that allows redistribution. Pick something with real bass,
vocals or guitar, and cymbals, so every tuning step is easy to hear.
About 60 to 120 seconds is plenty; the player loops it.

Record the title, artist, and licence of the track you add in the README.
